Features

Platform capabilities

Major functionality available throughout the Audisa platform.

Products can be categorized and maintained with product information, images, pricing, specifications and related colour options.

Sales teams can create quotations, add products, calculate totals and move approved business into the ordering workflow.

Contacts and customer information can be organized into a centralized CRM-style workspace.

Employees can record their working time through clock-in and clock-out functionality.

Internal and customer communication can be organized within the system rather than being distributed across disconnected tools.

Access can be organized around different users, account types, workspaces and business responsibilities.
