About Healthway Cancer Care Hospital

A dedicated destination for cancer care.

Healthway Cancer Care Hospital is located in Taguig City, Metro Manila and is described by Healthway as the Philippines' first comprehensive and dedicated cancer hospital.

The hospital provides a spectrum of cancer services ranging from screening and diagnosis to treatment and post-cancer care, with an emphasis on an integrated patient experience.
